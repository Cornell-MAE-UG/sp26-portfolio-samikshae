Given: The average force required to crack a macadamia nut is about 490 lbf, the nut diameter is 1 in, and the typical maximum hand grip force available is about 80 lbf.

Find: Determine the free-body diagram when the nut is about to crack and the required dimensions of the nutcracker so that a human hand force can generate enough cracking force.

Approach to solve: Explode the nutcracker into free-body diagrams, apply moment equilibrium to relate the lever arm distances, then use an average hand width constraint to solve for the final dimensions.

Usability of design: The nutcracker design is highly usable because the linear actuator provides a consistent force well above the 80 lbf required, eliminating reliance on human grip strength. The actuator’s 3.1-inch stroke is sufficient to fully close the jaws around a macadamia nut, making the device easy to operate and reliable for repeated use.
